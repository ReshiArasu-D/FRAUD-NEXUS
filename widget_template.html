How do I report fraud?

Click "Report Fraud" in the sidebar and follow the 7-step wizard. You can report payment fraud, UPI scams, identity theft, and more.

How does Chain of Custody work?

Every piece of evidence uploaded to FRAUDNEXUS is logged in an immutable, append-only custody ledger with timestamps and user identification.

What is the National Cybercrime Helpline?

In India, call 1930 immediately to report active financial cyber fraud and request a freeze on fraudulent transactions.

How is my data isolated?

Your records are protected with ServiceNow Access Control Lists (ACLs). Only you and assigned investigators can access your case.
